Tasks due today or overdue join "Give me ONE thing" and get priority (it can still pick anything). Uses the Todoist connector in your Claude account; Claude asks permission the first time.

Get it in Todoist: Settings, Integrations, Developer. It is stored only in this browser and is not included in backups.

Exams and deadlines come from Todoist tasks with these words in the name (comma-separated). A task on the same date as a built-in exam keeps that exam's chapters.
